Four faces, four jobs.

10 · Rules of the road

What "alive" actually means here.

Five rules that keep every future screen — landing page or dense dashboard — recognizably part of the same system, in either mode.

1. Neutral stays neutral

The canvas and ink scale never carry a hue. If a background looks tinted green, blue, or cream, it's wrong — that tint is what flattened v3's dark mode. Color belongs to the accents, not the base.

2. One glow per surface

A card can have a colored shadow on hover, or a colored icon chip, or a colored badge — pick one focal point of color per card, not three competing for attention.

3. Motion always springs

Hover and click transitions use ease-spring, never linear. A 3–4px lift plus a soft glow is enough — resist animating everything at once.

4. Modules keep their color forever

Sales is always coral, inventory always violet, accounting always teal. Once assigned in the sidebar icon, that color follows the module into charts, badges, and empty states.

5. Numbers are mono, everything else isn't

Any figure a user might compare — money, dates, IDs — renders in Geist Mono with tabular numerals. Labels, buttons, and prose stay in General Sans.

6. Say what happened, plainly

"Profit up Rs 53,544 today," not "Great news!" Confidence comes from specificity, not exclamation points — that's what makes the product feel alive without feeling loud.
