# Getting Started with VenQore

Welcome to VenQore POS! This Q&A guide walks you through setting up your account, selecting a plan, and navigating the central dashboard.

## Questions & Answers

### Q: How do I create a new VenQore account?
**A:** Setting up your account takes less than two minutes:
1. Visit the [Registration Page](/register).
2. Enter your full name, business email, and a secure password.
3. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the activation link.
4. You will be redirected directly to the Store Creation Hub to set up your store name and currency.

### Q: Which pricing plan is right for my store?
**A:** Select the core engine size based on your location and catalog counts:
- **Starter Engine ($36/mo):** Ideal for single-location shops. Includes offline POS checkout, 3 staff accounts, 1,000 product SKUs, the double-entry accounting ledger, and email support.
- **Growth Engine ($63/mo):** Ideal for expanding stores. Unlocks multi-location sync (up to 3 branches), 10 staff accounts, 10,000 SKUs, batch & expiry tracking, recipes (BOM), bank reconciliation, and live agent chat support.
- **Enterprise Engine ($129/mo):** Designed for multi-channel operations. Unlocks 10 locations, 50 staff, 50,000 SKUs, serial/IMEI tracking, automated assembly production runs, customer loyalty systems, and custom gift cards.

### Q: How does the 14-day free trial work?
**A:** Every new store starts with a **14-day free trial** of your selected tier:
- **Instant Activation:** Your trial starts immediately with full access to all features of your selected tier.
- **Trial Expiry:** You can cancel anytime before the 14 days conclude from your dashboard settings.

### Q: How do I navigate the main sections of the dashboard?
**A:** Once logged in, your central menu guides you to:
- **POS Terminal:** The main checkout screen for ringing up sales. Runs offline and automatically syncs when online.
- **Sales & Purchases:** Where you record customer invoices, manage supplier purchases, and track customer credit debt.
- **Inventory Control:** Used to manage products, variants, print barcodes, and manage warehouse transfers.
- **Accounting & Ledger:** The heart of VenQore. Shows your automated journal entries, Balance Sheet, and Profit & Loss.
