VenQore Design Language

A quiet operating system for the business of running a business.

CATEGORY
Offline-first retail operating system — POS, inventory, manufacturing, accounting, BI.
PRIMARY USER
A 54-year-old shop owner at 9:40pm reconciling the till. Not a power user. Not patient.
DESIGN MANDATE
Survive fifteen years without a redesign. Reduce cognitive load before adding beauty.
STATUS
v1.3 — three internal critique passes applied. See Critique & Iterations.

This document is normative. Where it uses must, deviation is a bug. Where it uses prefer, deviation requires a written reason in the pull request. Tokens are the only legal way to reference a color, a radius, a duration, or a shadow — literal values in product code are rejected in review.

Section 01 · Brand Philosophy

VenQore's brand is not a logo or a color. It is the feeling of a shopkeeper closing the till at 9pm and knowing the numbers are right. Every visual decision serves that single emotional outcome: your business is under control — communicated without ever saying it.

PRINCIPLE MEANING IN PRACTICE ACCEPTANCE TEST
Numbers first Figures get the strongest typographic treatment on any screen. Decoration never outranks data. Squint: do you see the number?
Legible at arm's length Counter staff read a 24" monitor from a metre away, standing, in fluorescent light. Readable at 70% zoom out?
Truthful state Offline, syncing, stale, draft, posted, locked — always visible, never inferred. Can the user be surprised?
Reversible by default Undo beats confirmation dialogs. Confirmation is reserved for money leaving the business. Count the modals: fewer?
Keyboard is the fast path Every repeated operation has a shortcut; the mouse is the fallback, not the design target. Full sale, no mouse?
Age gracefully No effect that dates the product: no gradient meshes, glass stacks, neon glows, or 2020s illustration. Plausible in 2010 and 2040?

Section 02 · Signature Color System

Three families, no more. Quarry — mineral neutrals that carry 92% of every screen. Cypress (#114A42) — one deep green for primary action and brand. Brass (#8A6A1E) — one metallic secondary for intelligence, value and attention.

FAMILY HEX CSS VAR USAGE RULE
Cypress 700 #114A42 --vq-cypress-700 The only fill permitted on a primary button in light theme.
Brass 500 #8A6A1E --vq-brass-500 AI suggestions & insights. Used at most twice per screen.
Quarry 0 (Paper) #FAF9F6 --vq-quarry-0 Light theme paper ground. Stone warmth that reads as paper, not beige.
Quarry 950 (Dark Ground) #121210 --vq-quarry-950 Dark theme ground, never pure black to prevent eye halation.
