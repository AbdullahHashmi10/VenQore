# SALES.md — How to Sell VenQore, Step by Step

## 0. Sales model by segment
| Segment | Motion | Price path | Close mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-store SMB (global) | Self-serve | Trial → Starter/Growth | Product does the selling; email drip closes |
| PK/South-Asia shops | Founder-led + resellers | PKR plans / self-hosted | WhatsApp demo → migration concierge → close |
| LTD hunters | AppSumo campaign | $49–179 stacked | Deal page + reviews + comments |
| Multi-store / mini-chains | Founder demo | Business plan/annual | 30-min demo + pilot store |
| Agencies (Woo/e-comm) | Partner program | Rev-share | Partner page + demo tenant |
| Accountants/CAs | Referral channel | Client pays; CA gets 20% | Statement/export demo |

## 1. Website conversion path (self-serve)
1. Landing → demo store (no signup — exists; instrument it) → trial signup (14d, no card).
2. Setup wizard must reach first sale <10 min; concierge import CTA on day 1 ("send us your Excel/Vyapar backup, we import free" — DataImportService + mapping UI exist; productize as a service touchpoint).
3. Drip (MARKETING §3) + in-app checklist + plan-gate upsell modals.
4. Day-13 offer: annual = 2 months free (coupon engine exists).
5. Card fail/dunning → LemonSqueezy retries → grace → view-only (never delete) → 90-day win-back email with WB50 coupon.

## 2. Demo script (founder, 30 min, multi-store & partners)
1. (2 min) Their pain first: "how do you know today's profit?" — silence is the pitch.
2. (5 min) POS: scan → 3-item sale → 6 keystrokes → receipt.
3. (5 min) THE MOMENT: open P&L → today's line → drill to journal entry → drill to that exact receipt. "Your accountant sees this same number. Nobody typed it twice."
4. (5 min) Unplug the internet. Make a sale. Reconnect. Watch it sync. (Rehearse this until boring.)
5. (5 min) Their #2 pain: inventory (expiry batches for pharmacy/grocery; serials for mobile shops; recipes for food).
6. (5 min) Import their real Excel live if provided (rehearsed path).
7. (3 min) Price anchor vs their current stack + AMC math vs Marg/Tally; close on pilot: "one store, 30 days, we migrate you free."

## 3. Objection handling (write these into Vena KB too)
- "My data is in Vyapar/Excel/Loyverse" → free concierge migration (templates exist), show a completed import in the demo.
- "Internet dies here" → offline demo + self-hosted option (DRM channel) for the paranoid.
- "My accountant uses Tally" → statements + exports today; Tally bridge on roadmap; accountant referral fee makes the accountant an ally.
- "Too cheap to be serious / too new" → hourly self-audit + reversal-only ledger explainer page; data export always free; show CHANGELOG velocity.
- "What if you shut down?" → export-anytime + self-hosted escape hatch — no rival offers this; say it proudly.

## 4. AppSumo campaign selling (60 days)
Answer EVERY question in the Q&A within 4h (ranking factor + trust) · daily comment digest ritual · reviewers get roadmap-vote invite · stack-upsell education: in-app banner "add 1 code for 2,000 tx/mo" · post-campaign: LTD cohort webinar + add-on catalog email (AI + channels are your LTD monetization).

## 5. Directories & listings (do once, week 1)
G2, Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice (one Gartner Digital Markets profile feeds all three), AlternativeTo (list as alternative to Loyverse/Vyapar/Square/Marg — traffic goldmine), SaaSHub, Product Hunt (ship page pre-launch), Crunchbase, StackShare, Techjockey + SoftwareSuggest (India/PK buyers actually use these), Indie Hackers product page.

## 6. Enterprise-ish (mini-chains, 5–20 stores) — only inbound, year 1
Pilot-store method: never sell 12 stores; sell 1 pilot with success criteria (30 days, defined reports match their old numbers), then expand on their proof · pricing: Business plan × stores, annual, 15% multi-store discount via override panel (exists) · require: their data import, 2 training calls (record → becomes docs), named support channel · contract: annual invoice via LemonSqueezy or bank transfer (PK reality: bank transfer + manual license via SuperAdmin — StoreLicense supports it).

## 7. Renewals & expansion
Renewal risk signal = login decay + sales-entry decay (Growth Engine can flag; wire a weekly "at-risk tenants" platform report) · expansion levers: staff-limit ceiling hits (upgrade prompt), channel add-ons after first Woo sync succeeds, AI add-on after 10th manual receipt entry ("SmartCapture would have done this") · annual-switch campaign at month 4 of healthy usage · win-back: view-only stores get quarterly "your data is still here + what's new" email.

## 8. Sales metrics
Demo→pilot ≥ 40% · pilot→paid ≥ 70% · self-serve trial→paid ≥ 25% (with import) / ≥ 12% (without) · AppSumo refund ≤ 12% · time-to-first-sale median <10 min · expansion MRR ≥ 15% of new MRR by month 6.
