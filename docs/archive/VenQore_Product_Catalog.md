## Section 2: Accounting Accuracy & Quality Assurance

VenQore has been put through an extensive, multi-layered audit process to ensure its accounting is completely reliable. Below is a plain-language explanation of what each verification level does and why it matters for your business.

### 5 Layers of Financial Verification

- **Layer 1 — Journal Integrity:** All financial records can only be written through one controlled gateway. Direct database tampering is blocked at the system level, preventing any accidental or unauthorized data changes.
- **Layer 2 — Live Balance Calculation:** Account balances are computed directly from raw transaction records, not stored as pre-calculated numbers that can drift out of sync. What you see is always mathematically current.
- **Layer 3 — Unified Reporting Engine:** A single, consistent calculation engine powers all reports. There is no separate calculator for the dashboard vs. the profit report vs. the balance sheet — they all read from the same source, so they always agree.
- **Layer 4 — End-to-End Scenario Testing:** 13 comprehensive real-world scenarios are tested automatically — including split-payment sales, purchase returns, and multi-currency transactions — confirming the entire system works together correctly.
- **Layer 5 — Dashboard & Statement Alignment:** Dashboard summary numbers are verified to match the detailed general ledger exactly, including correct return calculations and gross profit figures.
