AI usage

Models cost money to run. We'd rather show you the meter.

Blueprint, SmartCapture, Vena and Signals use AI models. Rather than hide that inside the plan price and quietly raise it later, we make it visible. One page of a document is one credit. Three photos of an invoice is three.

Included

Every plan includes a monthly AI allowance that covers normal use: building your system, changing it, and everyday captures. You see the count before you spend it.

Top up

200 extra pages for $2, any time. It is a one-off purchase, not a change to your subscription — and we stop at your cap rather than billing you past it.

Bring your own key

Connect your own model provider key and pay them directly. We do not mark up a key you supply. One-time unlock, then free for as long as you use VenQore.

We never silently truncate and never silently charge. When a 14-page PDF will use 14 of your pages, the screen says so and asks once.
