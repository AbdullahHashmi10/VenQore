# LAUNCH.md — Channel-by-Channel Launch Plans & Timeline

> Master sequence: Website/soft launch → AppSumo (60-day campaign) → Product Hunt (mid-campaign) → sustained channels. Preconditions: ROADMAP 0–30d complete (security/CI/backups), 20 pilot stores live, reviews seeded.

## T-30 days
- Site: pricing PKR toggle QA, demo-store instrumentation, comparison pages live (≥6), signature "unplug the router" video produced, docs site + Vena KB filled from pilot questions, status page up.
- Listings: G2/Capterra/AlternativeTo/SaaSHub/Techjockey/SoftwareSuggest profiles complete; PH "ship" teaser page; 10+ pilot reviews requested.
- AppSumo: deal terms locked (tiers = current $49/$99/$179, caps + 2-yr hosting disclosure in plain type), listing assets (10 screenshots, 90-sec video, FAQ), select committed 60-day support coverage plan.
- Email: list warm-up sequence begins (newsletter hub); segment: pilots, waitlist, personal network.
- Ops: load test signup + POS burst (k6: 200 concurrent trials, 1k sales/min), Sentry alert thresholds, on-call = founder + phone.
- Product Hunt: maker profile aging, hunter conversations (optional — self-hunt is fine in 2026), gallery/tagline drafts.

## T-14
Warm the network: personal DMs list (100 names, staged), pilot stores commit to launch-day comments/reviews · finalize launch coupons (PH25, SUMO stack guide) · schedule 8 weeks of post-launch content · freeze non-critical deploys · run restore drill once more.

## T-7
Rehearse demo + video embeds · AppSumo Q&A canned answers (20 predicted questions from SALES §3) · write launch-day posts (LinkedIn/X/FB groups/Reddit variants — value-first framing per community rules) · confirm AppSumo go-live date/time · brief pilots with copy-paste-able (but personalizable) snippets.

## T-3
Staging→prod final deploy + smoke tests (suite exists — run it) · scale plan: queue workers ×2, DB connection headroom check · support macros loaded · sleep.

## AppSumo LAUNCH DAY (Day 0)
Founder in Q&A every 2h (04:00 PKT overlap with US morning matters) · respond <4h all day 1–3 (ranking + refund prevention) · in-app welcome flow for sumo-lings (redemption UX exists — watch Sentry on `Redeem.jsx` path) · post to r/appsumo + deal-tracker channels · email list: "we're live" with honest terms · monitor: redemption rate, activation rate, refund reasons live-tagged.

## Campaign weeks 1–2
Daily: Q&A digest ritual, fix-fast on friction (CHANGELOG discipline continues publicly — sumo-lings love visible velocity) · YouTube deal-channel outreach (10 channels, affiliate codes via LemonSqueezy) · week-2: first "what we fixed from your feedback" update post (drives second wave).

## PRODUCT HUNT (mid-campaign, a Tue/Wed/Thu, 12:01 AM PT)
Assets: tagline "The POS whose books are always right", first-comment = founder story (solo + AI agents + why double-entry matters — genuinely differentiated), gallery incl. drill-down GIF + offline GIF · day-of: 6-hour sprint focus, staged personal DMs (no vote-rings), respond to every comment, LinkedIn/X live updates · goal: top-5 of day = permanent badge + backlink; conversion goal: PH25 coupon redemptions tracked · post: "what we learned" post at +7d.

## Weeks 3–8 (campaign back-half)
Case study #2–3 from LTD users · stack-upsell campaign in-app · affiliate recruitment from reviewers · comparison-page SEO indexing check · PK soft-launch events: 2 market-association demos (Karachi/Lahore), dealer conversations begin.

## Month 3 checkpoint (honest gates)
- ≥ 800 LTD units OR ≥ $6k cash+MRR → proceed to GROWTH stage plans.
- Refunds >15% → stop acquisition, fix top-3 refund reasons, only then resume.
- Activation (first sale in-app) <50% of redemptions → onboarding sprint before any new channel.
- Support >30 tickets/day sustained → hire PK support NOW (budget from LTD cash).

## Channel appendices (persistent programs, not events)
- **Reddit:** weekly value posts (inventory math, FBR explainers); AMA in r/smallbusiness after PH badge.
- **LinkedIn:** founder 3×/week; company page reposts; accountant-targeted carousel monthly.
- **YouTube:** 20-video tutorial library by month 3; Urdu playlist priority; every video pinned-comment → free tool.
- **Email:** weekly Retail Numbers; automated lifecycle drips (SALES §1).
- **Communities/Discord:** open a customer Discord/WhatsApp community only when >200 active stores (before that it's a ghost town — use a WhatsApp broadcast + group per city instead, which fits PK behavior).
- **Facebook groups:** 20 PK trader groups, 2 value posts/week, city-group demos.
- **Influencers:** PK business YouTubers (5–50k subs) — free Business plan + affiliate; global: bookkeeping/Excel creators for the "receipt→books AI" angle.
- **Affiliates:** LemonSqueezy affiliate program on from day 0; 30%/20% terms; asset pack page.
