# AI Growth Engine & Reports

Learn how to utilize our AI-powered automation features to scan invoices, predict sales patterns, and read profit reports.

## Questions & Answers

### Q: How do I use the AI Scan (SmartCapture) feature to process invoices?
**A:** SmartCapture allows you to scan paper invoices or receipts using your device's camera:
1. Go to the Sales or Purchase screen and click "SmartCapture Scan".
2. Position the invoice in the camera frame and click "Capture", or upload an existing PDF/image.
3. VenQore's AI OCR parses the image, extracts the vendor name, date, invoice number, line items, quantities, and costs.
4. Review the extracted draft invoice. Any new products can be added to your catalog with a single click.
5. Click "Confirm and Post" to automatically update inventory counts and write the accounting ledger entries.

### Q: How does the 4-brain AI Growth Engine work behind the scenes?
**A:** VenQore's AI Core utilizes four specialized routing layers to optimize your retail operations:
- **Catalog Intelligence Brain:** Automatically groups products, generates SEO descriptions, and suggests optimal categories based on scan history.
- **Forecasting Brain:** Analyzes purchase histories to predict inventory levels, preventing stockouts during seasonal demand.
- **Risk & Churn Brain:** Identifies inactive credit clients (Khata accounts) and prompts cashiers to send friendly WhatsApp reminders.
- **Anomaly Detection Brain:** Reviews ledger postings and alerts you to duplicate expenses or input errors.

### Q: How do I read and customize the Profit Intelligence Reports?
**A:** Go to Reports > Profit Intelligence. These reports use your double-entry transactions and FIFO cost depletion to show true margins:
- **Net Profit Margin:** Visualizes revenue minus COGS, operating expenses, and taxes.
- **Item-Wise Profitability:** Shows which products generate the highest net profit margin vs. raw sales volume.
- **Dead Stock Analysis:** Lists items that haven't moved in 60+ days, indicating tied-up capital.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Ranks customers based on their purchase frequency and average cart values.
