## Cross-reference to prior 7-doc audit

- The prior audit's **report-key/route-count figures ("39+4=43 vs 52")** are superseded by this pass's precise recount (48 seeded keys; 68 actual routes; 17 ungated) — see `PART_C_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_SWEEP.md` §4.
- The prior audit's **open question on `PlanFeature` model usage** is now closed: confirmed unused for runtime gating (`PART_D_CLOSE_GAPS.md` §1).
- The prior audit's flagged **frontend prop-namespace inconsistency** (`store.features` vs `plan.features`) is now shown to also affect **sidebar nav** (`PART_D_CLOSE_GAPS.md` §3), not just the previously-checked pricing/billing pages — a wider blast radius than previously documented.
- The prior audit treated **"226+ features" as a single unverifiable number**; this pass found it is actually three unrelated numbers (226+, 255+, 32) shipping simultaneously, and traced 226+'s origin to a specific commit (`PART_C` §1a, `PART_D` §5).
- The prior audit did not check **dashboard card-level gating** or **API route gating** at all — both are new surfaces covered for the first time in this pass (`PART_A`, `PART_D` §2).
- The prior audit did not identify that **`Tester/` is CI-load-bearing** — this is a new, process-critical finding: any future "exclude duplicate/test folders" instruction must not blanket-exclude `Tester/` from consideration when reasoning about what's authoritative, even though its *contents* were correctly out of scope for this content-focused audit.

## Top-line severity distribution

- **Critical (3):** LTD Tier 3 price drift ($50), LTD Tier 1 price drift ($20), `Billing/Index.jsx` fallback subscription-price table (off by $14–$30) — all real, customer-facing dollar-amount disagreements between `config/pricing.php` and frontend hardcoded fallbacks.
- **High (7):** AI add-on tier drift; triple/quadruple feature-count disagreement; report route/seeder reconciliation gap (17 ungated routes, inconsistent legacy-vs-V3 gating); dashboard card gating inconsistency across 7 files; permission/plan-entitlement architecture with no shared enforcement guarantee; frontend fail-open/fail-closed mismatch; `routes/api.php` zero plan-gating (especially WooCommerce webhook exposure); sidebar nav's three-mechanism inconsistency.
- **Medium (5):** `outstanding_balance_grid` undocumented-but-pattern-consistent Counter restriction; `Dashboard.jsx` grid layout gap on card hide; unused `PlanFeature` model as a 4th silent "feature list"; `Tester/`-vs-`FinalTester/` ambiguity; unverified "what's included" checklist parity.
- **Low (4):** per-employee permission toggle UI (informational, confirms existing capability); industry template count (no duplication found); "40+ reports"/"'1,500+ tests" claims (internally consistent, just unverified against ground truth); uptime/"Nx faster" claims (confirmed absent).
