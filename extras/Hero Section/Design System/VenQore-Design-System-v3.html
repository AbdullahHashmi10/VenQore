01 · Foundations

Color that carries a pulse

One saturated brand teal, two energetic accents, and a warm paper canvas instead of clinical white or dead black. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Brand — Signal Teal
Primary actions, focus states, links. The color people will learn to associate with "VenQore did this."
Coral — energy accent
Secondary CTAs, highlights, the "look here" color.
Sunshine — warmth accent
Achievements, streaks, gentle emphasis — never errors.
Ink & Canvas
A warm paper canvas, not clinical white — and text with a faint green cast so it sits with the brand instead of fighting it.
Semantic
Status colors stay vivid — a "healthy" chip should look healthy.
Module wayfinding
Same idea v2 had — one color per business area — turned up from a whisper to an actual signal. Lives in sidebars, tags, and empty states; never inside a data value itself.
02 · Foundations

Four voices, one sentence each

A display face with personality, a body face that disappears into legibility, a mono face that makes numbers feel counted rather than guessed, and one italic word per page as a signature.

Display — Bricolage Grotesque
Headlines, hero copy
Bold, a little irregular, unmistakably not the default SaaS sans. Used for anything meant to be felt before it's read.
Body — Inter
UI copy, paragraphs, labels
The workhorse. Every button, input, table cell and nav item. Chosen so it never competes for attention.
Data — JetBrains Mono

Flourish — Instrument Serif
one word, italic
Exactly one accent word per page, matching the landing page's voice. Never a whole sentence — that's how it stays special.

Motion is the tell

v2 banned hover states outside of media thumbnails — that's exactly why it read as dead. Here, everything interactive answers you: a lift, a spring, a soft glow. Nothing loops forever except three background blobs, and reduced-motion turns all of it off.
