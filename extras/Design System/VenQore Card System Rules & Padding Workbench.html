Rule 1 (C1 Exclusivity): C1 is exclusively designated for Shortcut action buttons. Analytical metrics, numbers, and stats start at C2 (Strip).
Rule 2 (Layout Law Mechanism): A card starts at the fit its author designed. If the column is narrow it gets wider; only when widening is exhausted does it degrade to a leaner fit, which trades a column for a row and re-lays its inside. That guarantees zero content clipping across any screen size.
