The rules that survive from v2

One primary per viewTwo primaries is no primary. The mint glow is reserved for it.
One focal card per screenExactly one mint-filled card. Everything else is a white floater or the single ink card.
Never scale on hoverButtons lift 1px, cards 2px, sidebar items change colour only. The one legal scale-plus-clip is an image inside a fixed-ratio frame.
No meaning in colour aloneStatus = icon + word. Negative = sign + parentheses + danger. Passing = tick + word.
Tabular figures everywheretnum globally; money columns line up or the product looks careless.
Twelve z-index values, no morebase 0 raised 10 sticky 100 nav 200 rail 300 dropdown 400 drawer 500 modal 600 popover 700 tooltip 800 toast 900 command 1000
Clipping is not stackingIf something is invisible, look for an overflow:hidden ancestor before touching z-index. Portal tooltips and dropdowns to body.
Tables have no vertical linesThe point is that you replaced the spreadsheet.
Never animate a ledger figureCount-ups belong on marketing stats, not on a balance.
Ambient motion is marketing-onlyAbove the fold, ≥768px, and it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
No emoji, everColour, shape and motion carry the playfulness.
