Compose your register

The last version shipped six fixed layouts. That was the wrong shape, and you were right about why — a register is composed by the person standing at it, not chosen from a menu of six. So the six survive as starting points, and every knob behind them is yours: where the catalog goes and how much room it gets, how the cart and the tender split, whether the tender is a column, a bar or a button. Drag the dividers in the screen itself. The law's job is no longer to pick a layout — it is to stop yours from breaking.

What changed, and why

Every one of these came out of your review of the last build.

What was wrongWhat it does now
Scan: below a certain height the money detail sat too far down — you could not hold the sale, open the drawer or change the method. A pane now pins its actions and scrolls only its body, so Complete, Hold and Drawer can never be pushed below the fold. When the tender has no column it becomes a dock button that opens the same panel full screen — same controls, every time.
Column on a phone and a tablet: the catalog ate the screen and you could not see what was being added. A catalog is only ever a resident column above 1182px of viewport — derived, not chosen: it needs 1062px of content to sit beside a full cart and a tender. Below that it is one button, full screen, with a live in-cart count on every tile.
Counter: the Browse-catalog button covered the payment panel. Triggers are no longer floated over the panes. The dock is a real layout row and its height is subtracted before anything else is measured, so it cannot overlap anything by construction.
1280 was a 33/33/33 split. Presets now carry your proportions — catalog 20 · cart 50 · tender 30 — and the fractions are normalised so sliders can never sum past 100%.
Grid was 50/50. 40 catalog / 60 cart, with the tender as a full-screen sheet behind Take payment.
You liked the idea of catalog above, cart below, pay on a button. That is now its own preset — Stack. The band snaps to whole tile rows so a 40% share that only buys one row gives the rest back to the cart.
Table put too much above the order. The floor is a step, not a fourth column competing for width. Switch it to a column in the composer if the screen can carry one for free.
Heights felt over-managed. Nothing is removed to save height any more. Panes scroll; the only vertical rule left is that the cart keeps first claim, measured against the height that survives the dock.

What the composer exposes

Worth knowing what the category actually offers here: Toast lets you set the menu grid's rows × columns per device, Lightspeed lets a tile span 1 to 30 spaces, Loyverse has a grid/list toggle, and Shopify and Square let you edit tile contents only. Nobody ships resizable panes. The one product with free pane geometry is Dynamics 365 Commerce, where layouts are authored per-resolution in an admin tool and exported as XML — not dragged at the register.
