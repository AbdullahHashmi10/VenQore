Two stacked 1-row cards must line up with one 2-row card

The bug Gemini flagged. Left: the gutter is added between cards, so a 2-row card is 64+64=128 while two stacked 1-row cards are 64+24+64=152 — a 24px misalignment that cascades down the page. Right: the gutter is part of the pitch, so a 2-row card spans across it and absorbs it: 2×64 + 1×24 = 152. The red line marks where the two columns should agree.
