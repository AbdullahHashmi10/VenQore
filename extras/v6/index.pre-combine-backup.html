Core Ledger

AI decides what your system looks like.
It never decides what your numbers say.

Every module — sales, purchases, stock, payroll, expenses — posts through one double-entry engine. The AI can add a module, rename a field, rewire a workflow. It cannot invent a balance. Debits equal credits, or the transaction does not post.

One engine, every module

There is no second version of the truth to reconcile, because there is no second version. Your P&L is not a report someone generated — it is the same data your till produced, read from the other end.

Verified, not asserted

Balance integrity, cost of goods, tax handling, multi-currency, inter-branch movement, period closing, reversal integrity. Seven independent correctness checks, run on every release.

Auditable by design

Every posting traces back to the document, the user and the timestamp. Nothing is edited in place — corrections are reversals, the way accounting is supposed to work.

Most vendors ask you to trust that their accounting is right. We'd rather publish the check.
