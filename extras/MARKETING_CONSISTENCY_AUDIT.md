## Root observation

The two confirmed-wrong claims (Growth Engine, WooCommerce) are not really "stale marketing numbers" in the traditional sense (a feature count that drifted after code changed) — they are **structural misrepresentations that were true according to a different, competing source of truth** (`Billing/Index.jsx`'s hand-maintained `FEATURE_UPGRADE_TARGET` map, `WhatIsIncluded.jsx`'s hand-maintained per-tier boolean props) that never agreed with the backend seeder to begin with. This is a duplication problem wearing marketing-copy clothing — see `ROOT_CAUSE_ANALYSIS.md`.

The "40+ reports" and (unverifiable) "226+ features" claims are the more classic form of marketing-copy risk: a number typed once by a human, with nothing in code enforcing it stays true over time. Even where currently accurate (reports), there's no mechanism preventing drift.
