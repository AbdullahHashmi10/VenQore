## C. The Sales Sprint (daily, 4h, non-negotiable)

**Math:** 10 customers ≈ 40 demos ≈ 200 conversations ≈ ~35 personalized touches/day. Track touches daily.

**Where to hunt (international first):**
- **WooCommerce owners** — FB groups ("WooCommerce Help & Share" etc.), r/woocommerce, WooCommerce community Slack. Pitch = the two-inventory problem; the sync is LIVE.
- **Loyverse users** — Loyverse community forum + FB groups. They chose free POS and hit the no-accounting wall. Pitch = "your register and your books, one system."
- **Square fee-payers** — r/smallbusiness, retail owner groups. Pitch = fee math ($500K sales → ~$13,000/yr in Square fees vs $432/yr VenQore Starter).
- **CPAs/bookkeepers with retail clients** — LinkedIn search "accountant retail small business". Offer the free multi-client dashboard; one CPA = many clients.
- **Vyapar users** (revenue, mostly PK/IN) — migration pitch, 10-minute .vyb import.

**The pitch (memorize, from `04` §F):** never list features. Send the demo: *"Ring up a sale in our live demo — no signup. Watch the stock change and the profit update at the same moment, from the same numbers. That's the whole idea: one entry, everything done."* Then offer a 15-min personal walkthrough on their products.

**The close:** founding-member annual — 12 months for the price of 10, price locked for life, direct founder WhatsApp support. Annual cash now > monthly trickle. Ask every demo (buyer or not): *"Which two shop-owner friends should see this?"*

**Follow-up cadence (5 touches):** Day 0 value message → Day 2 LinkedIn connect → Day 5 case/fee-math email → Day 9 "made you a 2-min walkthrough" (Loom) → Day 14 founding-offer deadline. All templates in `04`, section H.
